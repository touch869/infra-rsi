# Experiment learnings

Generalizable findings from completed experiment steps. Keep individual run
details in `experiment_log.md`; retain reusable conclusions here.

## Entry format

For each finding record:

- the triggering symptom and why it matters;
- workload, topology, runtime, and tensor shapes;
- the change mechanism and affected code path;
- correctness, engagement, and performance evidence;
- end-to-end result and measurement convention;
- applicability limits and known counterexamples;
- conditions that would justify a retry;
- links to the experiment record and raw evidence.

Do not promote a single run to a general rule without stating its evidence and
scope.
